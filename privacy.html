Personal Brand Manager: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

What this is

Personal Brand Manager is a personal tool made and used by one person ("the owner") on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does with TikTok

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token and basic account identifiers for the owner's own account. It uses them only to upload the owner's own, already edited video files to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner then adds the caption and sound and publishes each video in the TikTok app. The tool never publishes anything by itself and never acts on any other person's account.

What is stored and where

Access tokens and the app's client credentials are kept only on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on a server operated by the owner. Video files stay on the owner's computer until they are uploaded to TikTok.

What is not done

  • No data about other TikTok users is collected.
  • No data is sold, shared or sent anywhere except to TikTok through its official API.
  • No advertising, tracking or analytics tools are used.

Ending access and deleting data

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions, and can delete the stored tokens from the computer.
